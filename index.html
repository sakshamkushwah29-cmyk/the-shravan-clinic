Where Care Begins

Mental Health Care That Starts With Understanding

Mental health concerns can affect how we think, feel, work, study, and connect with others. Getting the right support starts with understanding what you are experiencing.

Dr. Apoorv Shah is a specialist Neuro-Psychiatrist, De-addiction Specialist and Certified Psychotherapist (M.B.B.S., M.D., D.N.B. Psychiatry, Reg. MP-24632) at The Shravan Clinic, Ujjain. He provides personalised psychiatric assessment and evidence-based care across different stages of life.

From depression, anxiety, OCD, and de-addiction to bipolar disorder, sleep issues, childhood ADHD and autism, care is tailored to each individual's needs with both modern pharmacotherapy and psychotherapy.
