Mental Health Care That Starts With Understanding

Mental health concerns can affect how we think, feel, work, study, and connect with others. Getting the right support starts with understanding what you are experiencing.

Meet Dr. Apoorv Shah

M.B.B.S., M.D., D.N.B. PSYCHIATRY | Reg. No. MP-24632

Dr. Apoorv Shah (Reg. No. MP-24632) is a mental health professional skilled in diagnostic assessment, psychopharmacology, and a certified therapist. He completed his M.B.B.S. from GMC SSMC Rewa, M.D. in Psychiatry from Chirayu Medical College & Hospital Bhopal, and D.N.B. in Psychiatry from NBEMS. Proud founder of The Shravan Clinic, Ujjain.

He serves as Assistant Professor at Abdul Kalam Institute of Medical Sciences, Indore, and former Senior Resident at Chirayu Medical College & Hospital, Bhopal. His research on antidepressant discontinuation tapering protocols and substance dependence has been published in international peer-reviewed journals. He is NIMHANS ECHO certified in Addiction Psychopharmacology and American Heart Association BLS & ACLS certified.

His clinical experience includes Consultant Psychiatrist at Patidar Hospital Ujjain, Kasturba Hospital BHEL Bhopal, LakeCity Hospital Bhopal, Saharsh Hospital Ujjain, and Medical Officer at District Hospital Ujjain. He is a Life Ordinary Member of the Indian Psychiatric Society (LOM/A07/26).
