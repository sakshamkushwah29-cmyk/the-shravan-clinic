Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about our psychiatric services, appointments, and care protocols

What conditions does Dr. Apoorv Shah treat at The Shravan Clinic? +
Dr. Apoorv Shah specializes in comprehensive neuro-psychiatry, de-addiction medicine, and psychotherapy. We treat Depression, Anxiety Disorders, OCD (Obsessive Compulsive Disorder), ADHD in children and adults, Bipolar Mood Disorder, Childhood Behavioral Difficulties, Sleep Disorders, and Relationship/Family Stress.
How do I book an appointment with Dr. Apoorv Shah? +
You can book an appointment easily by calling us directly at +91 92326 43041, messaging us on WhatsApp, or clicking the "Book an Appointment" button on this website.
Where is The Shravan Clinic located and what are the clinic hours? +
The clinic is located at Sunshine Medicare, Near Tower Chowk, Freeganj, Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh 456010. Clinical consultation hours are everyday from 6:00 PM to 9:00 PM.
What happens during my first consultation? +
During your initial consultation, Dr. Apoorv Shah conducts a detailed clinical evaluation of your current concerns, emotional history, sleep and physical symptoms, and personal background. Together, an evidence-based personalized treatment plan (including therapy, lifestyle guidance, and medication if necessary) is created.
Is my consultation and health information confidential? +
Yes, absolutely. All patient consultations, clinical records, and discussions are kept strictly confidential in accordance with medical ethics and standard healthcare privacy guidelines.
Do all patients require psychiatric medications? +
No. Medications are only prescribed when clinically indicated and necessary to stabilize neuro-chemical imbalances. Many individuals benefit primarily from psychological therapies, counseling, behavioral modifications, and lifestyle interventions.
